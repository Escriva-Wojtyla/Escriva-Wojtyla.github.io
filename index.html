Fizeau displacement interferometer

Final year project · in progress · report due Dec 2026
Problem
Measuring very small displacements needs an optical setup read out by electronics that are precise and low-noise, and that can also drive the moving element.
What I built
An STM32 interface that samples four photodetector channels, streams them to a computer over USB, and drives a piezo amplifier from a DAC. I am also designing the amplifier and low-pass filter stage between the optical pickup unit's photodetector and the ADC. Firmware is in progress and the hardware is still being bench tested.
